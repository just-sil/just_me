P-09 Hardware + cloud

Telegram → ESP32 OLED

Messages, photos and video on a 128×64 display

Send a message, photo or short video to a Telegram bot and it appears on a tiny 128×64 OLED. A Cloudflare Worker stores the latest message, an ffmpeg service converts media into dithered 1-bit frames, and the battery-friendly ESP32 checks in every few minutes.
